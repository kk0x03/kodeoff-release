Terms of service

Last updated: 2026-10-21. These terms apply to the no-signup file-sharing channel of the Kodeoff website (the share page and its download pages); the channel stopped accepting new uploads on 2026-10-20, and these terms remain in force during the natural expiry window (24 hours) of existing links. Data handling for the apps and the account relay is covered by the privacy policy; this page and the privacy policy together form the conditions of using this channel.

No-signup sharing was a free, temporary channel, now paused: no new uploads are accepted. The rules before the pause: upload a single file without an account and get a link valid for 24 hours with at most 20 downloads.

1. Prohibited content

Sharing any of the following through this channel violates these terms:

Content violating any applicable law, including child sexual abuse material (zero tolerance, and it will be reported to authorities);

Malware: programs designed to harm devices, steal data, or disrupt systems;

Pirated content infringing others’ intellectual property;

Content violating others’ privacy, including publishing identity or location information without consent;

Any other content the operator judges to endanger the service or others’ rights.

To reduce malware risk, this channel refuses files with the following extensions outright (regardless of actual content; trailing dots or spaces are refused too): .exe, .scr, .cpl, .msi, .bat, .cmd, .jar, .docm. The list may change with the security landscape; this page is the authoritative publication. Anonymous files are never previewed inline in the browser — downloads are always served as attachments.

2. Reporting & takedown

Every download page offers a “Report abuse” entry; the reporting guide explains what to include and how to contact us.

Process: report received → operator verifies → takedown and file deletion → technical measures such as rate limiting or blocking for repeat abusers. The operator handles reports as fast as online time allows; no fixed response-time commitment is made.

3. Termination & disclaimers

The operator may pause, restrict, or terminate the no-signup sharing channel at any time; the operator has no obligation to proactively review shared content.

Links are valid for 24 hours from creation with at most 20 downloads; after expiry, exhaustion of downloads or traffic quota, files are deleted from the server and cannot be recovered — the anonymous pool has no backups. Do not send your only copy of an important file through this channel.

Kodeoff is the provider of a transport channel and temporary storage, not a publisher of the content; download pages state “shared by the sender via Kodeoff”, and no warranty is made as to the truthfulness or legality of user-shared content.

4. Jurisdiction & contact

The no-signup sharing service runs on Aliyun servers in Tokyo, Japan (ap-northeast-1).

Reports and abuse complaints: abuse@kodeoff.com (see the reporting guide for the status of this mailbox).
