Files and clipboard,
moving between your devices.

Sign in to the same account and files, folders, and clipboard text leave your Mac, pass through the relay you are signed in to, and land in the inbox on your iPhone, Android, or browser. The relay is a server that holds content until your other devices pick it up: the official hosted service requires an invite code to register, or you can self-host your own.

01 · Device transfer

Sent from your Mac,
received on any device.

Sending goes through the radial menu or the context menu; receivers sync on open. Check the size before sending big folders.

Open the web inbox →
Macfiles · text · screenshots→Relay→Phone / webinbox

Send files and folders

Send a single file from the radial menu or the context menu; folders are zipped automatically, with progress shown for the scan, compression, and upload stages. Above a threshold (default 500 MB, adjustable 100–2048 MB) a confirmation appears before compression starts: size, file count, and estimated time — cancel and nothing is queued. Receivers can preview after download; files and caches are managed per device.

Send clipboard text manually

Choose “Send clipboard to phone” from the Mac menu bar to send text and links. Up to 64 KiB per send; larger content is refused with a notice, never truncated. Plain text is never synced in the background.

Auto-send after screenshots

Once a screenshot is finished and copied, it can be sent automatically to the clipboard tab of your phone’s inbox. This switch is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Screenshot; sending is skipped when not signed in. Only the latest auto-synced screenshot is kept per sending device, and a failed upload keeps the previous one.

Android receiver

Sign in with email or account code and pair devices with a pairing code; the inbox receives text and image deliveries with incremental sync and per-download SHA256 verification; the links tab lists your own shares and can revoke them; clipboard images can be sent too. The app polls while in the foreground — messages arrive the next time you open it.

AirDrop for nearby devices

On the Mac you can call the system AirDrop with multi-file selection — no Kodeoff relay account needed.

Transfer runs through the relay of the account you signed in to: the official service (invite-code registration) or your own self-hosted one; a network is required and end-to-end encryption is not live yet. The iOS and Android receivers sync while the app is open (Android polls in the foreground, no background push); the web inbox refreshes in the foreground.

02 · Link sharing

One link
sends a file to anyone.

Create a share link in the app and the recipient opens it in a browser to download — no account needed on their side. Creating a link requires signing in; the no-signup web upload channel has been paused since 2026-10-20, and links created before that expire naturally within 24 hours.

Both sharing methods explained →

Live channel

In-app share links stay

After signing in to your relay account, create links from the Finder context menu, the screenshot preview, or screenshot history — recipients open them in any browser and download without an account. Link validity and remaining downloads are shown on the actual download page.

Channel status

No-signup web uploads are paused

The no-signup share page has been replaced by a pause notice: it no longer accepts new uploads; links created earlier still follow the original rules — valid for 24 hours and up to 20 downloads, after which files are deleted from the server automatically.

Abuse reports

Reports for existing links still accepted

If an existing link contains illegal, infringing, or terms-violating content, report it via the reporting guide; the handling process is in section 2 of the terms of service.
